FREQUENTLY
Asked Questions
What is the dress code?
We'd love for our guests to dress in cocktail or formal attire. Think garden-party elegance — florals, pastels, and classic suits are all welcome. Please avoid wearing white or ivory out of respect for the bride.
Are children welcome?
We love your little ones! However, we've chosen to make our wedding an adults-only celebration so everyone can relax and enjoy the evening. We hope you can join us and treat it as a wonderful night out.
Is there parking at the venue?
Yes! Rosewood Retreat has ample complimentary parking on-site. We also encourage guests to consider carpooling or using a rideshare service if you plan on celebrating with a glass (or two!) of champagne.
Can I take photos during the ceremony?
We kindly ask that you keep phones and cameras away during the ceremony so you can be fully present with us. Our photographer will capture everything beautifully. After the ceremony — snap away!
Are there hotel accommodations nearby?
We've reserved a block of rooms at The Grand Pinebrook Hotel, just 5 minutes from the venue. Use code EMMAJAMES28 when booking for a special rate. Details available on our Travel page.
What if I have dietary restrictions?
Please let us know your dietary needs in your RSVP. We'll have vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options available, and our caterer is happy to accommodate other allergies with advance notice.
